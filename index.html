How this runs

  • Answer, and the item is marked straight away — you cannot change it, same as thinking it through properly the first time.
  • Every option gets a verdict, not just the right one. Knowing why B fails is what the exam actually tests.
  • Each item cites its BABOK v3 task, so you know exactly what to re-read.
  • Questions and options are shuffled each session, so retaking a set is not a memory test.
  • Untimed, but the clock counts up — useful, since the real exam allows about 105 seconds per item.
  • The end-of-session summary breaks your score down by knowledge area.
