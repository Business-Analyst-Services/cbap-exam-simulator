BABOK® Guide v3 — a twelve-week programme

The whole guide, in the order a study group works it. Each week explains the ground, names what the exam tests, and drills it.

Twelve weeks covers the BABOK Guide v3 end to end: the key concepts that hold it together, the six knowledge areas in the order that makes them easiest to learn, and the perspectives that sit behind them. Each week is written to be read before a session and used during one.

Every week names the tasks in scope, the concepts worth being precise about, the techniques that belong to that session, and the traps the exam sets. Then it drills you on exactly that ground, and hands you blank templates for the techniques so you can use them on real work rather than only recognise them in a question.

The tools

Practice questions A full 120-item timed sitting, a coached study session over any knowledge areas, or a week from the plan. 208 original items. Technique lab All 50 BABOK techniques: what each is for, what it is confused with, and the artefact it produces — drawn, not described. Templates Every technique as PowerPoint, Word and Excel — a worked example and a blank template each, 300 files. One scenario, end to end A single engagement worked through sixteen techniques, each step taking what the one before it produced.
